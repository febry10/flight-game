Sky Courier — a 3D arcade flight game: fly a little orange cargo plane around an island archipelago, thread glowing rings, mind your fuel and the storm clouds, and land back on the runway to bank your score. Three sorties (Island Hop, Parcel Run, Storm Circuit) with different ring courses, timers and weather. Chase camera, live instruments (speed, altitude, heading, throttle, fuel, damage), minimap chart, synthesised engine/wind/ring sounds, keyboard + touch controls. Runs entirely offline in a desktop browser; Three.js is vendored under game/vendor/.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click a sortie and fly: W/S pitch, A/D bank, Shift/Ctrl throttle, Space airbrake, P pause, M mute.
